# TEKTON — Brand voice

**Tagline:** The Operating System for Construction.

**Positioning:** One connected workspace for construction projects, people, procurement and costs.

**Personality:** Structured, reliable, precise, modern, quietly premium.

**Tone:** Clear without jargon; confident without unsupported superlatives; practical and respectful.

**Recommended example:** “Bring projects, people, procurement and costs into one clear workflow.”

**Avoid:** “Revolutionary!”, “world’s only”, guaranteed savings, or implying simulated features already operate with real company data.

**Important:** TEKTON is a provisional name until construction-software trademark clearance; do not use ™ or ® in production logos.
