# TEKTON — Typography and interface specifications

Primary font: **Inter**, with Arial / generic sans-serif fallback. Do not distribute font binaries with the kit.

| Role | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Hero H1 | 48–64 px, Bold 700, line-height 1.08–1.16 |
| Section H2 | 28–40 px, Semibold 600, line-height 1.18–1.25 |
| Body | 15–18 px, Regular 400, line-height 1.5–1.7 |
| Captions | 11–13 px, Medium 500, line-height 1.4 |
| Buttons | 13–15 px, Semibold 600 |

Radius scale: small 6 px, medium 10 px, large 16 px, pill 999 px. Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 px.

Primary action: Charcoal background `#1F1F1F`, white text. Canvas: Concrete White `#F5F5F2`. Card: pure white. Borders: `#DADADA`.

Contrast guide: Charcoal on Concrete White approx **15.1:1**; Graphite on Concrete White approx **4.9:1**; Ash on white approx **1.5:1**, insufficient for normal text. Do not use Ash for readable text.

Functional colours for warning, success and error may be used in product UI only if accessible. Brand graphics should remain neutral.
